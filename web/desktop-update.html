Installing over your current version

Quit Grok Build Desktop first, then run the installer. Your projects and conversations live in ~/.grok and are untouched by an update.

The download is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens straight after you replace the old copy — no warning, and nothing to allow through in Settings.

SmartScreen will show “Windows protected your PC” the first time: click More info, then Run anyway.
